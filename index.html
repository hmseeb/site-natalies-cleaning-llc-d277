Our Services

Cleaning built around how you actually live

Every home is different, so every clean is quoted to the room. Pick a recurring rhythm or a one-time reset — the checklist is thorough either way.

Recurring House Cleaning

Weekly, bi-weekly or monthly upkeep that keeps the whole house guest-ready. Same trusted cleaner whenever possible.

  • Dusting, vacuuming & mopping
  • Beds made, surfaces wiped
  • Kitchen & bathroom refresh

Deep Cleaning

The full reset. Built-up grime, neglected corners and the spots a normal clean never reaches get our slow, detailed pass.

  • Baseboards, door frames & vents
  • Blinds, ceiling fans & light fixtures
  • Behind and under movable furniture

Kitchen Detail

Where the grease lives. Stovetops, backsplash, cabinet fronts and appliance exteriors degreased and polished.

  • Stovetop, hood & backsplash
  • Cabinet fronts & handles
  • Inside microwave, fridge on request

Bathroom Sanitizing

Tile, grout, glass and fixtures scrubbed and disinfected until they shine — no lingering film, no shortcuts.

  • Showers, tubs & glass doors
  • Toilets sanitized inside & out
  • Mirrors, sinks & chrome polished

Move-In & Move-Out

Empty-home cleaning for closings, leases and landlords. Cabinets, closets and drawers cleaned inside and out.

  • Inside cabinets, drawers & closets
  • Appliance interiors degreased
  • Walk-through ready for inspection

Small Office Cleaning

Offices, studios and suites around Plainfield cleaned after hours so your team starts every morning on a fresh floor.

  • Desks, breakrooms & restrooms
  • Trash out, floors vacuumed & mopped
  • Flexible after-hours scheduling

Need something that isn't listed — post-construction dust, inside the oven, laundry day? Ask us in the form and we'll quote it.
